Research Program 01

Graph Stream Learning and Resource-Constrained Approximate AI

This research program develops principled algorithms for learning from dynamic graph streams in a single pass and under bounded memory, computation, and latency. It builds on my longstanding work in hashing, graph mining, adaptive learning, and scalable representation design.

The central idea is that real-time graph learning should not depend on storing full graph histories or retraining from scratch. Instead, we can design compact summaries and approximate representations that preserve the most useful structural and temporal signals for downstream learning.

Core capabilities

  • Single-pass graph stream summarisation
  • Adaptive hashing and approximate representations
  • Concept-drift aware graph learning
  • Robustness, reliability, and interpretability

Integrating reliability, uncertainty awareness, and interpretation into graph stream learning systems.

Research Program 02

AI Autism and Multimodal Neurodevelopmental Screening

My AI Autism research focuses on earlier and more reliable autism screening by integrating behavioural and brain-based signals with modern representation learning. This program is motivated by the need for screening tools that are both technically strong and practically meaningful for healthcare settings.

Recent work spans deep embedding methods for autism screening, multimodal neuroimaging frameworks for autism diagnosis, and a current multimodal AI agenda that brings together brain and behavioural signals for early screening. This program is also being developed through a close collaboration network that links autism-specific expertise, multimodal health AI, and community-facing translation.

Translation focus

  • Early screening rather than late retrospective analysis
  • Multimodal fusion across behavioural and neuroimaging data
  • Clinically useful representation learning
  • Trustworthy and inclusive healthcare AI

Behavioural screening AI

Learning representations from screening data to better support autism identification in real-world assessment pathways.

Multimodal neuroimaging

Deep multimodal frameworks that combine multiple neuroimaging signals to improve autism diagnosis.

Brain and behaviour integration

New work on integrating brain signals and behavioural indicators to support earlier, richer, and more robust screening decisions.

Healthcare AI translation

Extending machine learning research into clinically meaningful, interpretable, and deployable health analytics systems.
