Research Overview

Explore the work by theme.

The homepage stays concise. Detailed program descriptions, selected publications, and broader expertise are available on the Research page.

This program develops new methods for learning from fast-evolving graph streams under strict memory, computation, and latency constraints. The central idea is to place resource budgets at the centre of algorithm design rather than treating them as an afterthought.

Research questions

  • How can dynamic graph streams be processed in a single pass under bounded memory and latency?
  • Which compact graph representations preserve learning-relevant structure under bounded resources?
  • How can graph learning stay adaptive under concept drift and constrained computation?
  • How can graph stream learning remain robust, reliable, and interpretable in deployment?

Why this matters

The agenda supports responsible and energy-efficient AI for evolving networks in areas such as communications, finance, healthcare, transport, and public services.

My AI Autism research focuses on earlier and more reliable autism screening by integrating behavioural data, neuroimaging, and multimodal learning. The emphasis is on clinically meaningful models that can support real-world translation rather than isolated benchmark performance.

Current directions

  • Behavioural screening AI and learned representations for autism identification
  • Multimodal neuroimaging models for autism diagnosis
  • Integration of brain-based and behavioural signals for early screening
  • Trustworthy and inclusive healthcare AI for translational settings
